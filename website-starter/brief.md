# Brief

Answer what you can. **"I don't know yet" is a good answer**: it stays a visible blank in the draft,
and the build will not publish it as a live fact. Never guess to fill a gap.

Copy this file for each client. Claude reads it to fill `content/content.json` and to pick sections.

## 1. The business
- Name:
- What do you do, in one sentence?
- Who is it for? (be specific: who, in what situation)
- What is the ONE thing you offer? (one is stronger than a menu)
- Language of the site:

## 2. The visitor
- What is she/he feeling or struggling with when they find you? Use their words, not vocabulary from your field.
- What do they worry about before booking? (pain, cost, being judged, time, "is it for me?")
- What do they NOT say out loud, but recognise as soon as they read it?

## 3. The offer
- What happens in an appointment, step by step?
- What is it good for? (list the situations, not the technique)
- What is it NOT for, or what can't you promise?
- When should someone see a doctor first?

## 4. What you can prove (only things that are true and that you can show)
- Real reviews? Where are they (Google, Facebook, e-mail)? Can they be quoted, with or without a name?
- Training, certifications, memberships? (names and years)
- Years of experience, number of clients? (only if you can stand behind the number)

## 5. Practical
- Prices (single, series, gift card)? Or not decided yet?
- Address, parking, opening hours, phone, WhatsApp, e-mail:
- Do people come to you, or do you travel / work online?
- How do people book today? (booking tool such as Salonized, e-mail, phone)
- Where should form messages go (which e-mail address)?

## 6. Look and feel
- Logo and brand colours (files, hex codes)?
- Photos you have, and photos you don't have yet (owner portrait, workspace, the people you help):
- Three websites you like, and what you like about each (any industry):
- Three adjectives for how it should feel:
- Anything it must NOT feel like:

## 7. Sensitive information
- Does the site ask for health or other personal information? (Then you need a privacy page and a careful form.)
- Is there a disclaimer you must show (for example, that you don't replace a doctor)?

---

### How the answers decide what is built
| If you know… | Then… | If you don't… |
|---|---|---|
| a clear promise | it becomes the hero line | Claude drafts one from sections 1 and 2 and marks it for review |
| the visitor's own words | they become the numbered points and the FAQ | the points stay generic and are flagged |
| real reviews | the reviews section is shown | the section is left out of the live site |
| prices | the pricing section is shown | left out (or "on request" if you prefer) |
| training and credentials | they appear under "about" | those rows are left out, never invented |
| an address people visit | practical info and a map | online-only: only e-mail/phone, no map |
| a booking tool | its widget is embedded | the form is the booking route |
| a portrait of the owner | a photo in "about" | text only, no empty frame on the live site |
| sensitive topics | privacy link, disclaimer, careful wording | — (this one is always asked) |
