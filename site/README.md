# House of Dreambuilders: marketing site

A static site, separate from the app in the repo root. No build step.

- Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this folder: `npx serve site`
- Sign-up form: set `data-endpoint` on the `<form>` in `index.html` to your email service URL. Until then it shows "not connected yet" and sends nothing.
- Fonts load from Fontshare (General Sans) and Google Fonts (Geist Mono).
- Prices and release dates are a marked placeholder in the Products section.
