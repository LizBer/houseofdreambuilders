C-section cupping, in detail

Starts
Six to eight weeks after birth, once your doctor agrees.
Feels like
A gentle lift from soft silicone cups.
May ease
Pulling, tenderness, the firm ridge above the scar.

Questions mothers ask

Does cupping hurt?

It should not. You can ask for less at any moment.

Is my scar too old?

No. Scars from years ago can still be treated.

How many visits will I need?

It differs for every body. We talk it through at your first visit.

Is the support for the mind therapy?

No. It is listening, breath and rest. If you need more, we help you find the right professional.
