What cupping actually is

The cup
Soft silicone, small enough to sit in my palm. No fire, no glass, no bruising marks left behind.
The feeling
A gentle lift and stretch as I move it along the line, always at your pace.
When we start
Six to eight weeks after birth, once the scar has closed and your doctor agrees.
Older scars
Scars from years ago can still be treated, and often respond quickly.
