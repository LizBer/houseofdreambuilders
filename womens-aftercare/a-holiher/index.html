Before you come

When can I start after a caesarean?

Usually six to eight weeks, once the scar has fully closed and your doctor or midwife agrees. Before then we can still talk, and I will tell you honestly if it is too soon.

My scar is years old. Is that too late?

No. Scars from three years ago, or ten, still answer to this work, and older tissue often changes faster than women expect.

Does the cupping hurt?

It should not. You feel a lift and a stretch. You can ask for less at any moment, without explaining yourself, and I check as we go.

Can I bring the baby?

Yes. Babies come to almost every first visit. Feed, settle them, start again. Nothing about the hour has to be tidy.
