The weeks after birth

01 the first days

Week one

Everyone asks about the baby.

You are bleeding, leaking, aching and awake at four in the morning, and the questions all go past you to the cot. Nothing is asked of you here. Rest, and know the door is open when you want it.

Week two

The scar is still a wound.

Nothing touches it yet, not my hands and not yours. This is the week for lying down, drinking water and letting the incision close in its own time.

Week six to eight

We can begin.

Once the scar has closed and your doctor agrees, I can start the cupping. Soft silicone cups lift the tissue so the layers underneath learn to glide again.

  • It is a lift, not a squeeze. The cup draws the skin up gently and I move it slowly along the line.
  • You set the pressure. Say less and it is less, every time, without explaining yourself.
  • It may ease the pull. The tightness, the tenderness and the firm ridge above the scar.

Week twelve

You start carrying everything again.

The car seat, the pram, the toddler who wants up. Your back and shoulders take it, so this is where the massage work usually begins.

Any week after that

It is never too late.

Scars from three years ago, or ten, still answer to this work. So does a birth you have never told anyone about.

  • C-section cupping

    Soft cups free the tissue around a healed scar.

  • Postpartum massage

    Slow, warm work for your belly, back and hips.

  • Scar care lesson

    I teach you the strokes to use at home.

  • Your birth story

    Tell it from start to finish. I listen.
